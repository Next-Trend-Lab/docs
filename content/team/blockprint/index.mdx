## Project Overview

### Problem Statement

Nigerian university students face unresolved infrastructure and welfare issues due to poor visibility, weak communication, and lack of accountability.

### Solution Summary

**CampusRant** is a decentralized platform that elevates student voices, exposes real infrastructural problems transparently, and connects students' needs directly to sponsors driving tangible improvements on African campuses.

### Target Community

**Primary:**
- University students
- Campus ambassadors
- Student Union bodies (optional)

**Secondary:**
- NGOs and Foundations
- Alumni associations
- Brands (CSR departments)
- School administrators
- Journalists and media outlets
